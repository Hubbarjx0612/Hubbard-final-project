Vernon County Fair

September 16-21

Link to Website

The Vernon County Fair in Viroqua is a historic annual event held each September, featuring livestock shows, 4-H exhibits, carnival rides, live music, and classic fair food. Dating back to 1856, it celebrates the area’s agricultural roots while offering fun for all ages.

Admission
Adults (16+): $10 per day
Seniors (65+): $5
Kids (6–15): $5
Children (5 & under): Free
